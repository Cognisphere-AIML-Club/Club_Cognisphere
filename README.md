> **Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**  
> **Sambhram Institute of Technology (SaIT), Bengaluru**  
> *Presented by Cognisphere — AI & ML Club*

---

## 🌟 Overview

**IGNITE 2026** is the official web application and event companion for Sambhram Institute of Technology's premier annual technical and non-technical fest. Designed and built from the ground up by **Cognisphere (AI & ML Club)**, the platform delivers an end-to-end digital event experience for students, participants, and attendees across campus.

The application serves as the central hub for discovering competitions, accessing offline-ready digital entry passes, tracking live leaderboards, and instantly generating verified credentials and certificates.

---

## ✨ Features

### 1. 🎯 Event Discovery & Competition Catalog
* **Complete Event Guide**: Browse all technical and creative competitions, including **Tech Quiz**, **Prompt Decode (Reverse Prompt Engineering)**, **UI/UX Makeover Challenge**, and more.
* **Timeline & Stage Breakdowns**: Detailed schedules, multi-round stage progression, rules, and scoring criteria for every event.
* **Campus Venue Allocations**: Clear room assignments and start times for smooth physical navigation on event days.
* **Interactive Event Guides**: Comprehensive walkthroughs for participants detailing problem statements, software requirements, and evaluation guidelines.

---

### 2. 🎫 Smart QR Event Pass
* **Personalized Digital Pass**: Instant QR pass generated for every registered attendee with student identification and event allocations.
* **Offline Caching**: Built-in local caching guarantees passes stay accessible even in low-connectivity campus auditoriums and basements.
* **Real-time Status Badges**: Displays registration status, room locations, and verified check-in state.

---

### 3. 🏆 Live Leaderboards & Arena Podiums
* **Real-Time Scoreboards**: Instant, dynamic score and ranking updates as evaluations conclude.
* **Animated Winner Podiums**: Custom podium display highlighting 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place winners with medals and achievement trophies.
* **Big Screen / Arena Mode**: High-contrast, large-format display mode with custom projector typography (`Arkhip`, `Audex`, `SkateBlade`) engineered for auditorium stages and hall projections.

---

### 4. 📜 Instant Digital Certificates & Verification
* **Zero-Wait Client Generation**: Powered by high-resolution HTML5 Canvas rendering for instant generation directly on your device.
* **Official Fest Typography**: Rendered with **Glacial Indifference** typography matching the official institutional certificate design.
* **Dynamic Event Centering**: Automatically balances and centers event names of variable lengths with distinct regular and bold-italic styling.
* **Export Options**:
  * **High-Res PNG**: Crisp, print-ready image export.
  * **True-to-Scale PDF**: 1:1 vector-accurate PDF export preserving exact 3507 × 2480 dimensions with zero margin distortion or page clipping.
* **Public Authenticity Verification**: Every issued credential includes a unique Certificate ID and deep-link verification URL (`/verify?id=...`) allowing employers, universities, and LinkedIn connections to verify credential validity in real-time.

---

### 5. ⏱️ Interactive Competition Timers
* **Multi-Stage Round Timers**: Dedicated visual timers for time-boxed competition rounds with customizable durations.
* **Audio-Visual Alerts**: Smooth animations, milestone pulses, and audible stage cues to keep competitions on track.

---

### 6. 👤 Participant Profile & Identification
* **Smart USN Intelligence**: Automatic college branch and graduation batch year detection parsed directly from VTU USN (e.g., *1ST24AI...*).
* **Certificate Name Personalization**: Option to specify exact preferred name formatting for certificate generation.
* **Self-Service Overview**: Consolidated view of registered events, credentials earned, and event participation history.

---

### 7. 📱 Progressive Web App (PWA) & Mobile-First Experience
* **Installable App**: Add to home screen on iOS and Android for a native app feel without downloading from an app store.
* **Offline Resilience**: Service worker precaching for critical visual assets, fonts, and pass data.
* **Fluid UI/UX**: Motion-rich interface built with Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion spring physics, and modern glassmorphic accents.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

* **Frontend Framework**: [React 18](https://react.dev/) + [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
* **Styling & Design System**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)
* **Motion & Animations**: [Framer Motion](https://www.framer.com/motion/)
* **Database & Realtime Sync**: [Firebase Firestore](https://firebase.google.com/)
* **Certificate Graphics Engine**: HTML5 Canvas 2D API + [jsPDF](https://github.com/parallax/jsPDF)
* **PWA & Offline Worker**: [Vite PWA Plugin](https://vite-pwa-org.netlify.app/)
* **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
